Jingyao Gong
Ph.D. Student, Nanjing UniversitySuzhou, Jiangsu, China

I am a Ph.D. student at Nanjing University, advised by Prof. Wenbin Li in the RL-MIND Group. My research focuses on efficient AI models. I am also interested in large language models, including pre-training, post-training and reinforcement learning, and their extension to vision and speech.

I received my M.S. (2025) and B.Eng. (2022) from Jiangsu University, where I worked with Prof. Heping Song on image compressed sensing, and then worked as an algorithm engineer at Westlake Institute for Data Intelligence.
